What is the HCF?

HCF means Highest Common Factor.

It is the biggest number that divides both numbers with remainder 0.

Example: 12 and 18. These are all the numbers that divide them.

12
1234612
18
1236918

green = divides both numbers. 6 is the biggest one.

HCF of 12 and 18 = 6

For big numbers like 408 and 391, writing every factor takes too long. The tricks on this page are much faster.

Tool 1: Divisibility checks

These checks tell you if a number divides by 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 or 13 without doing the full division.

÷2

Last digit is even

Last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. Then it divides by 2.

126 → ends in 6
63 → ends in 3
÷3

Add the digits

Add all the digits. If the total is in the 3 times table, the number divides by 3.

126 → 1+2+6 = 9
134 → 1+3+4 = 8
÷5

Ends in 0 or 5

Last digit is 0 or 5. Then it divides by 5.

245 → ends in 5
134 → ends in 4
÷7

Chop and double

Remove the last digit. Double it. Subtract it from the number that is left. If the answer is in the 7 times table (or 0), it divides by 7.

343 → 34 − 6 = 28
134 → 13 − 8 = 5
÷11

Minus, plus, minus

First digit − second digit + third digit … If the answer is 0 or in the 11 times table, it divides by 11.

286 → 2 − 8 + 6 = 0
134 → 1 − 3 + 4 = 2
÷13

Just divide

For 13 and bigger, there is no quick check. Do the division. Remainder 0 means yes.

169 ÷ 13 = 13 r 0
134 ÷ 13 = 10 r 4

Tool 2: Two tricks for the HCF

🪜 Trick A: The Ladder

Good when the numbers are even, or end in 0 or 5.

284126
34263
71421
23
  1. Find a number that divides both. Check in order: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13.
  2. Write it on the left. Divide both numbers. Write the answers below.
  3. Do it again with the new numbers. Stop when no number divides both.
  4. Multiply the left side numbers. That is the HCF.

2 × 3 × 7 = 42. HCF = 42

➗ Trick B: The Remainder trick

Good for big numbers and odd numbers.

1,071÷462=2remainder147
462÷147=3remainder21
147÷21=7remainder0
  1. Divide the big number by the small number.
  2. Remainder is not 0? Make a new pair: the small number and the remainder. Divide again.
  3. Remainder is 0? Stop. The number you divided by is the HCF.

HCF of 1,071 and 462 = 21

⚡ Quick checks (do these first)

  1. Does the small number divide the big number? Then the small number is the HCF.
    36 and 144: 144 ÷ 36 = 4 r 0. HCF = 36.
  2. Subtract the numbers. The HCF always divides the difference too.
    408 and 391: 408 − 391 = 17. Check 391 ÷ 17 = 23 r 0. HCF = 17.
  3. Difference is 1? Then the HCF is 1.
    250 and 251: difference 1. HCF = 1.

Which trick do I use?

Both numbers are even, or end in 0 or 5🪜 Ladder
Numbers are big or odd➗ Remainder trick
Numbers are close together⚡ Subtract first

Both tricks always give the same answer. You can use either one.
